Beta feedback

Email volgaaytac@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, game name and steps to reproduce the issue. TestFlight feedback is also welcome.

Account and data deletion / Hesap ve veri silme

To request deletion of server-side account and score records, email the address above with subject “MiniVerse data deletion” and the online identity shown in the app’s Account screen. Do not send passwords, access tokens or recovery codes. We may need to verify the request before deleting records. This is a support request process; the app currently has no self-service account deletion button.

Sunucudaki hesap ve skor kayıtlarının silinmesini istemek için yukarıdaki adrese “MiniVerse veri silme” konusuyla yazın ve uygulamanın Hesap ekranındaki çevrimiçi kimliği belirtin. Parola, erişim token’ı veya kurtarma kodu göndermeyin. Silme öncesinde talebin doğrulanması gerekebilir. Bu bir destek talebi işlemidir; uygulamada otomatik hesap silme düğmesi yoktur.

Signing out removes local login credentials after server revocation; it does not delete online records. Local XP, settings and personal bests are separate from online records. You can remove device-local app data using iOS controls; Keychain behavior can differ from app data deletion.
